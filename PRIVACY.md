# Privacy Policy — Gym Forged

**Last updated: 2026-05-22**

---

## Summary

Gym Forged stores everything on your device. Nothing is uploaded, synced, transmitted, or shared. There are no accounts, no servers, and no analytics.

---

## What data is stored

All data is written only to your browser's `localStorage` on this device:

| Data | Where |
|------|-------|
| Workout history (sets, reps, weight, date) | localStorage |
| Exercise configuration (working weights, target reps) | localStorage |
| Progression state (clean sessions, rep bonuses) | localStorage |
| Settings (theme, rest timer, equipment profile, etc.) | localStorage |
| Check-ins (energy, soreness, sleep) | localStorage |
| Body metrics (optional: weight, body fat) | localStorage |
| Goals and user profile | localStorage |

---

## What data leaves your device

**None.** Gym Forged has no backend, no API calls, and no third-party SDKs. There is no internet connection required after the initial load.

---

## Backups and export

Use **Settings → Export** to download a full backup as a JSON file. That file stays on your device (or wherever you save it). Use **Settings → Import** to restore it. Backups are never sent anywhere automatically.

---

## Clearing data

- Clearing your browser's site data (cache + storage) removes all Gym Forged data permanently.
- Uninstalling the PWA from your home screen also removes local data on most platforms.
- There is no account to delete because there is no account.

---

## Third-party services

None. No analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, no CDN for fonts (all assets are bundled).
